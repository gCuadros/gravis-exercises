# Original demonstration workflow

No demonstration assets are included in this version. Empty media arrays and visible placeholders are intentional. Do not copy competitor GIFs or publish generated movement imagery without frame-by-frame technical review.

## Production standard

- Produce a short, clear demonstration of the exact named variation. Use consistent framing, lighting, background and wardrobe; keep the whole relevant movement and equipment visible.
- Prefer a controlled, silent MP4 demonstration with a still poster over a large autoplaying GIF. The current player has controls and no autoplay. Store large originals outside Git; attach versioned HTTPS delivery URLs to the exercise record.
- Show the start, controlled movement and return. A hold should show entry, stable position and exit. Do not hide critical contact points with branding or framing.
- Have a qualified reviewer check the full clip, movement endpoints and agreement with the written instructions. Visual polish is not technical approval.
- Obtain permission for the performer and media reuse. Record the actual creator, license and bilingual description. Keep consent documentation private; do not embed personal contact details in the public record.
- If animation or generative tools are used, treat their output as an unreviewed production draft. Approve the final result only after checking anatomy, equipment interaction and movement throughout.

## B001 Group A briefs

Eight additional briefs bring the total to 16. These are unapproved production specifications, not media assets. Use the stable record map and editorial references in [B001](batches/001-foundations.md#group-a-delivery-and-review-handoff); the record's bilingual instructions and counting conventions must agree with the eventual demonstration.

For each dynamic movement, capture setup, at least two uninterrupted complete cycles and a controlled finish. For the hold, capture entry, a clearly stable interval and exit. Record at normal speed; any slow-motion inset supplements the continuous take. Frame the full body and relevant equipment without obstructing contact points. Produce bilingual descriptions only after viewing the final clip; do not imply that a planned shot already exists.

| Record                          | Framing and action                                                                                                                | Technical review before approval                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `squat-bodyweight`              | Side/three-quarter view showing heels, knee height, hip position and return to standing; no jump or hand support                  | Confirm approximately parallel thighs and visible foot contact; show a consistent depth without losing trunk control             |
| `back-squat-high-bar-barbell`   | Three-quarter view with a side depth check; show high-bar position, full bar, rack hooks/safeties, unrack, repetitions and rerack | Verify upper-trapezius rather than neck/low-bar support; check hip-crease depth, rack clearance and both hooks receiving the bar |
| `deadlift-conventional-barbell` | Three-quarter view showing hand placement outside legs, entire bar, plates on floor, standing endpoint and reset                  | Confirm conventional stance, plate diameter/start height, controlled return and a visible pause/reset; no bounce or raised start |
| `bench-press-dumbbell`          | Foot-end oblique view plus side range check; show flat bench, both bells, supports, setup and finish                              | Confirm simultaneous motion, controlled bottom range and exit; any load overlay must show total mass and per-bell breakdown      |
| `pull-up-strict`                | Oblique view at bar height showing palms, chin, elbows and feet for the whole cycle                                               | Confirm pronated grip, hanging start, chin clearance and return; reject hidden leg drive, kip or assistance                      |
| `push-up`                       | Side/oblique view showing hands, chest clearance, hips, knees and toes through each repetition                                    | Agree on bottom clearance and arm extension; verify trunk/legs move together, knees stay clear and hands remain on floor         |
| `plank-forearms`                | Side view with both elbows and feet in frame; include entry, stable hold and lowering                                             | Match timer start to established alignment and end to first loss/contact; setup and exit must not inflate duration               |
| `single-under`                  | Full-body view with headroom and floor clearance for the entire rope arc; supplement with slow motion if passes are unclear       | Confirm exactly one rope pass per jump and two-foot landing; verify complete-cycle counts and interrupted-attempt handling       |

Record actual creator, permission, license and reviewer evidence after production. A useful open library requires reusable assets as well as attractive demonstrations; a brief or external reference supplies neither reuse rights nor approval.

## Attach an approved asset

Each media object needs `type`, `url`, `alt.en`, `alt.es`, `creator`, `license` and `reviewStatus`. Use `pending` during production. An approved still image alone does not satisfy publication: a reviewed video or animation is required. The initial website embeds video and links to animation assets; it does not generate or host them.

Review approval is a recorded human decision, not something the build can independently certify. Replace media URLs with versioned assets when changes affect technique, and return the exercise to draft while re-review is pending.
