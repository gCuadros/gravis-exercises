Do not use `ergometer_row` for resistance-training rows, `planche` for a forearm plank or one generic equipment key for all machines. A family groups variants without making their instructions or history interchangeable.

Muscle assignments must be independently proposed per record and reviewed; do not copy source muscle labels or force every movement into the pilot's 14 terms. Assess a clearly defined `spinal-erectors` term if required for the hinge/squat drafts, and any other justified omission. Broader vocabulary additions remain proposals until their semantics and translations are authored. Automated validity is not anatomical approval.
